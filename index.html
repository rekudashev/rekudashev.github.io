My research focuses on structural urban economics, using quantitative spatial urban models to study the economic impacts of cross-border labor mobility and taxation on housing markets, labor market outcomes, and individual well-being.

Publication · 2026

Floorspace Price Discontinuities and Taxation in Cross-Border Commuting Areas

with Pierre M. Picard · Journal of Urban Economics 155, 103904

Floorspace prices jump sharply at Luxembourg's borders with Belgium, France and Germany. Differences in tax rates and tax importing account for 8% and 13% of the jump; productivity and amenities explain the rest.

Welfare Effects of Congestion in Luxembourg and the Greater Region

with Pierre M. Picard

A spatial general-equilibrium model of Luxembourg and its cross-border commuting region, used to measure what congestion relief does to output, welfare and fiscal revenues.
