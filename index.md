Hello, and welcome to my website!

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Spatial Economics at VU Amsterdam and a candidate fellow at the Tinbergen Institute.

My research focuses on structural urban economics, using quantitative spatial urban models to study the economic impacts of cross-border labor mobility and taxation on housing markets, labor market outcomes, and individual well-being.

**Interactive tool — [Luxembourg Apartment Price Atlas](/luxembourg-atlas/).** Apartment price indices for Luxembourg's communes and cadastral sections (2007–2021), built with the Ahlfeldt, Heblich & Seidel (2023) method from land-registry sales, together with an interactive regression-discontinuity view of the price jump at the Luxembourg–Germany border. It accompanies Kudashev & Picard (2026), [*Floorspace Price Discontinuities and Taxation in the Cross-Border Commuting Region*](https://hdl.handle.net/10993/66030), *Journal of Urban Economics*; data sources and credits are listed on the atlas page. The atlas will be updated as more data from the cross-border areas become available.

Feel free to explore the site, and if you’d like to know more about my work, you can also check out my [CV](/assets/files/CV_2025.pdf).
